About SaviourCrest Hospital

Care you can trust, every hour of every day

For over two decades, SaviourCrest Hospital has combined experienced medical staff, modern facilities, and a genuine sense of care to look after our community — around the clock, every day of the year.

Our Story

Built around one simple idea

We started our journey by simple clinic at 1970, with only 4bedroom clinic and small Staff, with limited equipments and less and outdated machines. By Grace and Mercy Of Allah Almighty no we have constructed our spacious hospital wth more than 400+ bedroom hospital. Our hospital is recognized by international Medical Council also. We have imported most advanced machines which can treat patients with less pain.

SaviourCrest Hospital was founded with a single goal: make quality healthcare simple to reach, whatever the hour. What started as a small emergency clinic has grown into a full-service hospital with specialist departments, a dedicated blood bank, and digital tools that put patients first.

Today, we serve thousands of patients every month, supported by a team that treats every visit — routine or urgent — with the same level of care.

Our Hospital

Modern Healthcare Infrastructure

SaviourCrest Hospital features a modern, multi-block healthcare facility designed to support comprehensive medical services around the clock. Our expanded flagship campus spans extensive square footage, accommodating over 400 patient beds, dedicated surgical suites, and multi-story inpatient towers.

Engineered with accessible patient drop-off plazas, structured parking, underground ambulance bays, and well-lit exterior precincts, our infrastructure ensures smooth emergency intake and quick patient transfers at any time of day or night.

Every building is integrated with central climate control, specialized sterile filtration systems, and backup power networks, ensuring uninterrupted healthcare services in all conditions.
