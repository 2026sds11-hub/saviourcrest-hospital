Register for Blood Donation

Fill in your details below. Our blood bank team will reach out to schedule your donation at the nearest eligible slot.

Your information is stored securely in SaviourCrest Hospital's internal database. Once saved, your blood group's count on the Blood Bank graph updates automatically.

🩸 Urgently Needed

O-, A-, B- and AB- groups are currently low in stock. See live levels on the Blood Bank section.

✅ Eligibility Basics

  • Age 18–65 and in good general health
  • Weight at least 50 kg
  • No donation in the last 3 months
  • No active fever, infection, or recent major surgery

📍 What Happens Next

After you register, our blood bank team will call or email you within 2–3 business days to confirm eligibility and schedule your visit.
